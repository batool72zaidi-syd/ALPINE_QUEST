THE SEASON OF NEW BEGINNINGS

Discover Pakistan
in spring.

Spring brings Pakistan to life. As winter fades, valleys become green, flowers begin to bloom and mountain landscapes fill with color. From the famous blossoms of Hunza to the peaceful valleys of Swat, spring is a perfect time to explore the natural beauty of the country.

WHERE TO GO

Places worth visiting
in spring.

01
GILGIT-BALTISTAN

HUNZA

Spring transforms Hunza into a colorful paradise. Apricot and cherry blossoms bring the valley to life while the surrounding mountains create a spectacular backdrop.

WHY GO IN SPRING?

Perfect for photography, sightseeing, peaceful walks and experiencing the famous blossom season.

02
KHYBER PAKHTUNKHWA

SWAT

Swat becomes fresh and green during spring. Flowing rivers, blooming flowers and peaceful mountain scenery make the valley especially beautiful.

WHY GO IN SPRING?

Wonderful for nature lovers, family trips, photography and exploring beautiful green valleys.

03
PUNJAB

MURREE

Murree becomes pleasant and refreshing in spring, surrounded by green hills, beautiful trees and cool mountain air.

WHY GO IN SPRING?

Ideal for a relaxing getaway, enjoying nature and escaping the busy city.

04
GILGIT-BALTISTAN

BALTISTAN

After the long winter, the valleys of Baltistan begin to awaken. Green landscapes, clear skies and dramatic mountain peaks create an unforgettable spring setting.

WHY GO IN SPRING?

Great for scenic drives, photography, mountain views and discovering peaceful communities.
