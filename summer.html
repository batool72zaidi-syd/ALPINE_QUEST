SUMMER ADVENTURES

Find your perfect
summer escape.

Summer is one of the best times to explore Pakistan. While many cities experience warm weather, the northern mountains offer refreshing air, green valleys and unforgettable landscapes.

From Hunza and Skardu to Swat and the beaches of Karachi, there is something for every kind of traveller.

WHERE TO GO

Places to visit this summer.

GILGIT-BALTISTAN • 01

Hunza Valley

Visit Hunza for its cool mountain air, green valleys, peaceful villages and spectacular views of the Karakoram.

GILGIT-BALTISTAN • 02

Skardu

Summer is perfect for exploring Skardu's lakes, mountains, valleys and dramatic landscapes.

KHYBER PAKHTUNKHWA • 03

Swat Valley

Enjoy green mountains, flowing rivers, forests and refreshing weather in the beautiful Swat Valley.

PUNJAB • 04

Murree

Escape the heat and enjoy cool mountain weather, pine forests, scenic viewpoints and peaceful walks.

SINDH • 05

Karachi Beaches

Kundmalir Beach

Enjoy the Arabian Sea, seaside views and relaxing coastal moments at Karachi's beautiful beaches.

GILGIT-BALTISTAN • 06

Baltistan

Discover towering peaks, peaceful valleys and incredible mountain landscapes during the summer months.

02

BEACH ESCAPES

SUMMER TRAVEL TIP

Plan your adventure wisely.

Keep comfortable clothing, sunscreen, sunglasses and water with you. If you are travelling towards the mountains, carry a light jacket because temperatures can become cooler, especially at night.
